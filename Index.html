What is Blueprint?

Blueprint is the new prospective cordinating tool for teams of all sizes. The idea is that current teams , Slack all the messaging platforms lack one thing which is transparency and clarity which is what blueprints here to fix. Blueprint is going to be a platform where teams of all sizes can send notifications out for event planning post an RSVP for meetings allow team memebers to view avalibility and current issues in teams. Meant to be a mix of Gits useful notes and slacks to an all in one work communication platform.

Stay Updated!

What it does

Member roster

One accurate list of who's in your club. Members join in a tap and land on the roster automatically — no more guessing from a group chat.

Events RSVPs

Helps with planning either meetings or events. Say a dinner for a team is being held and you need to know how many allocations for the reservation or how much money is going to be used to buy the food at the event an RSVP event post can help save money and the hassle/

Announcements

The notifications can be used for announcements say actually announcing a company party or used to say a event is cancelled from weather or unforseen circumstance without contacting everyone one by one. This leads a papertrail of planning and helps avoid complications when it comes to etiquette for event plans.

Club directory (The big Idea)

College can be daunting especially for freshmen and things like joining a club can be intimidating to people new to thew college life opr people with social anxieties having a browse groups page could serve as a digital 24/7 club directory / involvement fair helping you know what clubs there are and what being in the club entails without the obligation of joining because of the social interations you had at the table for the club.
