What is Blueprint?

Blueprint is the new prospective cordinating tool for teams of all sizes. The idea is that current teams , Slack all the messaging platforms lack one thing which is transparency and clarity which is what blueprints here to fix. Blueprint is going to be a platform where teams of all sizes can send notifications out for event planning post an RSVP for meetings allow team memebers to view avalibility and current issues in teams. Meant to be a mix of Gits useful notes and slacks to an all in one work communication platform.

Stay Updated!

What it does

Member roster

One accurate list of who's in your team. Members join in a tap and land on the roster automatically — no more guessing from a group chat.

Events RSVPs

Plan meetings and events with a headcount. Know how many seats to reserve or how much food to buy before the day comes.

Announcements

Announce a party or call off an event for weather — without messaging everyone one by one. It leaves a paper trail too.

Club directory

A 24/7 digital involvement fair — browse what clubs exist and what they're about, with no pressure to join on the spot.
